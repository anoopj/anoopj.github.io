My 4th grader has been preparing for the
[Science Olympiad](https://www.soinc.org/) Simple Machines event, and we
wanted a fun way to review levers, pulleys, inclined planes, mechanical
advantage, and Newton's laws together. So I put together a small study guide
as a single-page HTML app:

[Simple Machines Study Guide](/simple-machines-study-guide.html)

It's one self-contained file with the notes, diagrams, and a few practice
questions, organized into sections you can click through. No build step,
no server, no dependencies. Just open it in a browser.

If your kid is also doing the Simple Machines event, feel free to use it.
